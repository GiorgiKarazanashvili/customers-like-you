# Individual Reflection

**Giorgi Karazanashvili**

I worked on this project alone, which is something I have been used to for a while. Preprocessing and cleaning data and setting it up for training is something I did quite a bit during my bachelor's, although most of those projects were either algorithm-based apps or individual machine learning projects. On the data engineering side, I cleaned the data, held out the last two months as a test period, and built the sparse matrix. On the ML side, I used SVD, chose k, built a recommender system, evaluated it on the held-out set, compared it with PCA, and produced a customer map splitting customers into four groups that marketing can target. I then wrote a memo for Sarah explaining the work and the reasons for my decisions and made a presentation showing the key results.

Most of the technical work was similar to projects I have done before. The key difference was the framing of the problem. I had never approached a project as if I were in an actual workplace: it was my first time writing a memo for a stakeholder, working with other teams such as marketing in mind, and following strict rules on how to handle the data. Most of my past projects gave me much more freedom in these choices.

What I would change is my timing. I unfortunately started working on the project on the 13th, which made me go over the deadline by a couple of hours. For the project itself, I would like to try other ways of choosing the number of customer groups, and to build a better system for handling new customers.
